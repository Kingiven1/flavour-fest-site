Good to Know
Getting around

Uber works fine and is the easiest way to move between venues. Taxis are common too — keep small cash on hand since not all take cards.

What to bring

Your ID, cash for tips, and a portable phone charger — you'll want your phone alive for the whole weekend.

Weather

Hot and humid with the chance of a quick afternoon rain shower — pack light, breathable clothes and don't stress if the sky opens up for ten minutes.
